Department of Civil Engineering · The University of British Columbia

Verifiable intelligence for how structures are designed, monitored, and built.

This page is a high-level visual overview of our ongoing work — intentionally leaving out implementation details to focus on the problems we care about and the direction we are working toward: auditable code-based design, non-contact 3D structural sensing, and safety-aware autonomous construction.

01

Offline Regulatory & Structural Design Brain

Engineering answers should never come from guesswork. We are exploring an offline workflow that reads real building codes clause by clause, delegates math and structural checks to dedicated tools, and enforces explicit verification before any answer or design is accepted.

02

Vision-Based Structural Health Monitoring (SHM)

Conventional structural monitoring relies on dense wired sensors at sparse points. We are developing non-contact, multi-view 3D tracking to capture full-field floor translation and torsion of large-scale structures under seismic excitation.

03

Autonomous Robotic Construction & Site Safety

To address skilled labor shortages and hazardous heavy lifting on construction sites, we integrate autonomous path planning with visual site understanding on a full-scale robotized mobile crane — enabling obstacle-aware construction, live progress reporting, and automatic pausing whenever workers enter active zones.

Related Publication Y. Xiao, H. Lin, T.Y. Yang — "Large language model and reinforcement learning based autonomous construction and progress updates," ACDSA 2026.
