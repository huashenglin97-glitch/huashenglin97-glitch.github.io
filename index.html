Research Focus

My work focuses on trustworthy, physically grounded intelligence for civil and structural engineering — connecting how buildings are designed against real codes, how they are built by autonomous machinery, and how they respond under extreme loads.

Selected Work

Offline Regulatory & Structural Design Brain

Ongoing

Our vision is to use large language models not as black-box answer generators, but as an auditable engineering orchestrator — reading real building codes, delegating mechanics and math to deterministic solvers, and iterating under a strict verification guard until a design is ready for engineer sign-off.

3D Multi-View Tracking for Structural Health Monitoring (SHM)

Ongoing

Non-contact multi-view 3D vision for large-scale shake table testing — tracking full-story translation (X, Y, Z) and floor torsion (rz) continuously under seismic shaking without dense wired sensor arrays.

Autonomous Robotic Construction & Site Safety

2026

Integrating autonomous lifting path planning with visual site understanding on a full-scale robotized mobile crane — enabling obstacle-aware retaining wall construction, automated progress updates, and automatic safety pausing when workers enter active zones.
